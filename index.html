    Signal tracker

    Every entry is paper-traded on live Deriv ticks: stop moves to break-even at +1R, exits at the playbook target or stop. Results are in R. This is your proof of whether the playbook has an edge.
    TimeMarketStrategySideEntryStopTargetResult (R)
    Deriv synthetic indices are generated by a random number engine and each tick is independent, so no rule can promise the next move. They have no news, no fundamentals, and Deriv designs them with constant volatility, so no method can promise to predict direction. This agent does what can honestly be done: it reads volatility regime, trend and stretch on several timeframes, only signals when every rule in a market-specific strategy lines up, sizes stops from ATR, and measures its own results in the tracker. If the tracker shows a win rate near or below 40 percent over many signals, there is no edge and you should not trade it with real money. Educational only, not financial advice.
